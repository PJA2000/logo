Putting it in Gmail

  1. Click Copy signature above.
  2. In Gmail, click the gear icon, then See all settings.
  3. On the General tab, scroll to Signature, click Create new, name it "Urban Atlanta".
  4. Click inside the box and paste (Cmd+V or Ctrl+V).
  5. Under "Signature defaults", pick it for new emails and replies, then hit Save Changes at the bottom.

If the Copy button gets blocked by your browser, drag to highlight the signature in the preview and copy it by hand. It pastes the same way.
